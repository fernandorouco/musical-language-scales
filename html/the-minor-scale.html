The minor scale

Besides the major scale, another very common scale is the minor scale. It also has seven notes and follows this pattern of intervals: T – ST – T – T – ST – T – T.

Now we are going to build it starting from la (A), forming the la (A) minor scale.

In the case of la (A) minor, we also use the white keys of the piano, just like in the do (C) major scale. This is not a coincidence. They are not parallel scales. Wait... what?
